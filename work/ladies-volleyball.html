Supporting the Aktive Sports 1st Ladies Volleyball League through sponsorship, community, and sport.

Event type
Sports event
Location

Date

ATOMY MEA – K4 Events proudly joined the Aktive Sports 1st Ladies Volleyball League as one of the event's sponsors, supporting a celebration of women's sport, teamwork, and community in Dubai.

The event

The league brought together talented ladies volleyball teams in a competitive yet friendly environment, creating an opportunity for athletes to connect, compete, and inspire one another.

Supporting the community

Through its sponsorship, ATOMY MEA – K4 Events supported a community sporting initiative focused on active living, teamwork, and the growth of women's volleyball in the UAE.

At the venue

The video captures Thomas, Manager of ATOMY, sharing his thoughts from the volleyball venue while teams compete in the background — highlighting the role of partnerships in bringing people together through sport and community.

Building connections through sport

The event brought athletes, organisers, sponsors, and supporters together for a memorable day of volleyball and camaraderie.
